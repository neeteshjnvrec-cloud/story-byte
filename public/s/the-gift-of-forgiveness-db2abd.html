Story

In a small temple village, there lived a monk who taught the power of forgiveness. One day, a traveler came with a heavy heart, burdened by anger and resentment. The monk gently shared a story of how holding onto anger is like drinking poison and expecting the other person to suffer. When the traveler finally let go of his grudges, he felt a weight lift from his soul. Forgiveness, he learned, is the greatest gift we can give ourselves.
