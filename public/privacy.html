Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2025

StoryByte ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and password when you create an account.
  • Profile Details: Phone number, gender, age, favorite genre, bio, and profile picture — all optional.
  • Usage Data: Listening history, stories played, listening time, favorites, and progress.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and app version for performance optimization.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the StoryByte experience.
  • Personalize story recommendations based on your preferences and listening history.
  • Track your listening progress, streaks, and favorites across devices.
  • Improve our app and develop new features.
  • Communicate with you about updates, new stories, and support.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase, which provides:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest.
  • Industry-standard security practices and compliance certifications.
  • Access controls ensuring only you can read your personal data.

Profile pictures are stored as Base64 encoded data within your user profile document.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your data is used solely within the StoryByte app to enhance your experience.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data at any time through the app.
  • Edit your profile information via the Edit Profile screen.
  • Delete your account — this permanently removes all your data from our active systems. Your data is archived briefly for integrity purposes, then permanently purged.

6. Children's Privacy

StoryByte is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy in the app and updating the "Last updated" date above.
